# Compute grants to onboard independent AI power and decentralise the network

Draft v4.1, October 2026. A twelve-month grant programme, funded by the Lightchain AI Foundation, for independent GPU workers and validators, with protocol fees repriced so the grants end as fees take over. The DAO Treasury spends nothing.

- [Community summary, 2 pages (PDF)](Lightchain-Compute-Grants-Community-Summary.pdf)
- [Full proposal v4.1 (PDF)](Lightchain-Compute-Grants-Proposal-v4.1.pdf) and [as Markdown](Lightchain-Operator-Viability-Proposal-v4.1.md)
- Eligibility script, probes and the public payment record: [lightchain-operator-program](https://github.com/lightchain-protocol/lightchain-operator-program)

Discussion: open for seven days from posting in this repository's Discussions, then to a Governor vote.
